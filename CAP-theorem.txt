Consistency - Availability - Partition Tolerance 

CAP theorem states that you can only have 2 out of the 3. It is required for defining non-fuctional behaviour of a system

Consistency means, the same data is shown over all the nodes/users.
Availability means, every requests gets a response (successful or not).
Partition Tolerance means, system works despite failure in between nodes.

Looking at this, partition Tolerance is usally a must needed factor out of the three. 

Hence the question will mostly be whether to choose Consistency or Availability.

Choose Consistency over Availability where the data is very vital to the system. ex: booking app, stock app, etc... Here the consistency of data is most important.
Choose Availability over Consistency where the data is not as vital to the system. Ex: In a social media platform, a stale comment is not a big deal rather than showing error.

-------- Things to Note ------------

-- Different part of a system can have different requirements. So, a part of a system can choose Consistency while the other can choose Availability.

In social media: 
  Profile details can choose Availability but the chat must be consistent.


--- Types of Consistency ---

-- Strong -> All reads should relfect most recent write
-- Causal -> related events should appear in order
-- Read-your-own-writes -> User should see their own updates
-- Eventual Consistency -> updates will propagate eventually
